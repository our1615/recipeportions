My Flour-Dusted Journey

I was a pastry chef at a Charleston hotel for eight years, scaling wedding cakes from 12 to 200 servings at 2 a.m. I went back to school for a math degree while working nights, then left the kitchen to teach culinary math at a community college. I realized home cooks were terrified of scaling. I built my first calculator to help my students, and it spread to home baker forums.

I live in a restored Victorian with a too-small kitchen, my husband, three kids, and a constant flour dusting on the counters. My 9-year-old can scale pancake recipes. My 6-year-old puts the cat in the mixing bowl. It's chaos. I love it.

Why I Built This Site

Every semester, students walk into my classroom convinced they "can't do math." By week ten, they're scaling soufflés for 50 people and explaining baker's percentage to their friends. The math was never the barrier. The barrier was fear.

RecipePortions.com exists to remove that fear. Every tool, every article, every calculator exists because someone asked me a question that shouldn't require a math degree to answer.
