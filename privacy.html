Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-15

1. Information We Collect

RecipePortions.com does not collect personal data through our calculators and tools. All calculations happen in your browser using JavaScript. We do not store your recipes, ingredient lists, or scaling factors on our servers.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We may use basic analytics tools to understand site traffic patterns. These tools may use cookies to track anonymous usage data such as page views and referral sources. No personally identifiable information is collected.

3. Third-Party Links

Our articles may contain links to third-party websites, including affiliate partners. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

4. Data Security

Because our tools process data locally in your browser, your recipe data never leaves your device. This is by design. We believe your kitchen data belongs to you.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@recipeportions.com.
